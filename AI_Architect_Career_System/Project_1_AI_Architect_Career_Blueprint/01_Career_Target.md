# Career Target & Role Definition

## Target
Senior AI Architect / Lead AI Architect.

## Positioning
Architect-level candidate with a strong combination of:
- Production GenAI and LLM application engineering
- Agentic RAG and multi-agent orchestration
- MCP/tool integration
- AWS, Kubernetes and MLOps
- AI observability and infrastructure reliability
- Python/API engineering

## Interview expectation
The target role should be approached at four levels:
1. Explain the concept clearly.
2. Implement a minimal working version.
3. Design a production-grade architecture.
4. Defend trade-offs under scale, reliability, security and cost constraints.

## Core interview dimensions
Architecture, technical depth, coding, productionization, cloud, reliability, security, leadership/communication, and business trade-offs.
