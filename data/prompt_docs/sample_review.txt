Product: UltraSound Pro Headphones
Rating: 4/5

The sound quality on these headphones is genuinely impressive — rich bass, clear mids, and crisp highs. I've used them for 8-hour work sessions and they're comfortable throughout. Battery lasts about 28 hours which is excellent.

The noise cancellation is effective in offices and cafes but struggles on planes. The companion app is clunky and took three attempts to pair. Customer support was helpful when I reached out but took 48 hours to reply.

Overall: great headphones let down slightly by average software and slow support.
