Version 2.4 | Effective Date: March 1, 2024

MESSAGE FROM THE CEO

At ACME Corporation, how we achieve results is as important as the results
themselves. Our reputation, built over decades, rests on a foundation of
honesty, fairness, and respect. This Code of Conduct is not merely a
compliance requirement — it is a practical guide for making the right
decisions every day.

Every employee, contractor, and board member is expected to read, understand,
and act in accordance with this Code. When in doubt, ask: "Would I be
comfortable if this decision were published on the front page of a newspaper?"
If not, reconsider.

— Jane Chen, Chief Executive Officer

1. SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY

This Code applies to:
- All full-time and part-time ACME employees globally
- Contractors, consultants, and temporary workers while engaged by ACME
- Board of Directors members
- Any third party acting on ACME's behalf

Subsidiaries and joint ventures in which ACME holds a majority interest
must adopt equivalent standards.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

A conflict of interest occurs when personal interests interfere (or appear
to interfere) with acting in the best interests of ACME.

2.1 Examples of Conflicts
- Holding a significant financial interest in a supplier, customer, or competitor
- Hiring or supervising a family member
- Accepting gifts or entertainment above permitted thresholds
- Using ACME resources (time, equipment, data) for personal benefit

2.2 Disclosure Requirement
All potential conflicts of interest must be disclosed to your manager AND
the Compliance team via the Conflict of Interest Disclosure Form on the
Ethics Portal. Disclosure alone does not authorise the conflicted activity —
Compliance will review and advise.

2.3 Outside Employment
Employees wishing to take on secondary employment, board positions, or
advisory roles at outside organisations must obtain prior written approval
from HR and their VP.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3. GIFTS AND ENTERTAINMENT

3.1 Permitted Gifts
Employees may accept gifts from external parties only if ALL of the following apply:
- The gift is unsolicited
- The value does not exceed $75 per occasion or $150 per person per year
- Accepting it would not create a sense of obligation or appear inappropriate
- It is not cash or a cash equivalent (gift cards, vouchers)

3.2 Entertainment
Business meals and entertainment with clients or suppliers are permitted if:
- They are reasonable, infrequent, and have a genuine business purpose
- A manager or business unit head pre-approves amounts exceeding $150/person
- They are fully expensed with a description of the business purpose

3.3 Giving Gifts
Gifts to government officials are strictly prohibited without prior
legal department approval, regardless of value.
Gifts to private sector customers or suppliers must not exceed $75 in value.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4. ANTI-BRIBERY AND ANTI-CORRUPTION

ACME has zero tolerance for bribery and corruption.
- Never offer, promise, give, or authorise anything of value to any person
  (government official or private individual) to obtain or retain business
  or to gain any improper advantage.
- Facilitation payments are prohibited even when they appear customary locally.
- All third parties acting on ACME's behalf (agents, distributors) must be
  screened and contracted with appropriate anti-corruption clauses.

Violations may result in criminal prosecution, significant fines, and
immediate termination. Report any suspicions to ethics@acme.com.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

5. FAIR COMPETITION AND ANTITRUST

ACME competes vigorously but always legally.
- Never discuss pricing, territories, customers, or production levels with
  competitors, even informally.
- Never agree with competitors to divide markets or boycott customers/suppliers.
- Comply with all applicable antitrust and competition laws in every
  jurisdiction where ACME operates.
- Contact the Legal department immediately if approached by a competitor
  about any of the above topics.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

6. INSIDER TRADING AND CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

6.1 Insider Trading
Employees who have access to material non-public information about ACME
or any other publicly traded company must NOT:
- Trade in that company's securities until the information is publicly disclosed
- Share the information with anyone who might trade on it (tipping)

Material information includes: earnings results, M&A discussions, major
contracts, regulatory approvals, or significant management changes.

6.2 Confidential Business Information
Do not share ACME's confidential information (trade secrets, customer data,
product roadmaps, financial projections) with unauthorised parties,
including family members and former colleagues.

Confidentiality obligations survive termination of employment.

7. WORKPLACE RESPECT AND NON-DISCRIMINATION

7.1 Equal Opportunity
ACME is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis
of race, colour, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, veteran
status, sexual orientation, gender identity, or any other characteristic
protected by applicable law.

7.2 Harassment-Free Workplace
Harassment of any kind — verbal, physical, visual, or through digital
channels — is strictly prohibited. This includes:
- Sexual harassment
- Bullying and intimidation
- Discriminatory jokes or comments
- Unwelcome physical contact

7.3 Reporting
Employees who experience or witness harassment should report it to:
- Their manager (if the manager is not the subject)
- HR Business Partner
- Ethics Hotline: 1-800-555-ETHICS (anonymous, 24/7)

Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is prohibited and
will itself be treated as a serious violation.

8. USE OF COMPANY ASSETS

8.1 Physical and Digital Assets
Company assets — equipment, software, data, vehicles, facilities — must be
used primarily for business purposes. Incidental personal use is acceptable
provided it is not excessive, does not harm productivity, and complies with
all IT and security policies.

8.2 Intellectual Property
Work product created during employment that relates to ACME's business is
the sole property of ACME. This includes inventions, code, designs,
written materials, and research.

8.3 Social Media
When posting on personal social media about topics related to ACME:
- Make clear that views are your own, not ACME's
- Do not disclose confidential information
- Do not make misleading statements about ACME's products or competitors
- Follow all applicable securities laws regarding forward-looking statements

9. REPORTING VIOLATIONS — SPEAK UP

9.1 Your Obligation to Report
All employees have a responsibility to report suspected violations of this
Code, ACME policies, or applicable law. You may report to:
- Your direct manager
- HR Business Partner
- Legal or Compliance department
- Ethics Hotline: 1-800-555-ETHICS (anonymous, 24/7, available in 15 languages)
- ethics@acme.com

9.2 Non-Retaliation
ACME strictly prohibits retaliation against any employee who reports a
concern in good faith or participates in an investigation.

9.3 Investigations
All reports are investigated promptly and confidentially. Employees are
expected to cooperate fully with investigations.

10. CONSEQUENCES OF VIOLATIONS

Violations of this Code may result in:
- Formal written warning
- Suspension with or without pay
- Termination of employment
- Civil or criminal referral to appropriate authorities

The severity of the consequence will reflect the nature of the violation,
intent, impact, and whether the employee was cooperative during the investigation.
