WELCOME TO YOUR BENEFITS

This guide summarises all benefits available to full-time ACME Corporation
employees. Part-time employees working 20+ hours per week are eligible for
a reduced benefits package (marked with * where applicable).

Enrollment deadlines, forms, and detailed plan documents are available at:
benefits.acme-internal.com

1. HEALTH INSURANCE

1.1 Medical Coverage
ACME offers three medical plan options through BlueCross BlueShield:

Plan A — High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP)
- Monthly premium (employee only): $85
- Monthly premium (employee + family): $310
- Annual deductible: $1,500 individual / $3,000 family
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $5,000 individual / $10,000 family
- Paired with a Health Savings Account (HSA) — ACME contributes $750/year

Plan B — PPO Standard
- Monthly premium (employee only): $195
- Monthly premium (employee + family): $580
- Annual deductible: $500 individual / $1,000 family
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $3,500 individual / $7,000 family
- No referral needed for specialists

Plan C — PPO Premium
- Monthly premium (employee only): $290
- Monthly premium (employee + family): $820
- Annual deductible: $250 individual / $500 family
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $2,000 individual / $4,000 family
- Comprehensive coverage with lowest co-pays

1.2 Dental Coverage
ACME covers 80% of preventive care (cleanings, x-rays) at 100% in-network.
Basic and major services covered at 70% after deductible.
Orthodontia benefit: $2,000 lifetime maximum for dependents under 19.
Annual maximum benefit: $2,500 per person.

1.3 Vision Coverage
Eye exam: $10 co-pay once per year.
Frames or contact lenses: $200 allowance per year.
LASIK discount program available through VSP network.

2. RETIREMENT AND FINANCIAL BENEFITS

2.1 401(k) Retirement Plan
- ACME matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of salary.
- Employees may contribute up to IRS annual limit ($23,000 in 2024).
- Vesting schedule: 25% per year, fully vested after 4 years.
- Investment options include target-date funds, index funds, and self-directed.
- Roth 401(k) option available.

2.2 Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP)
- Employees may purchase ACME stock at a 15% discount via payroll deduction.
- Two offering periods per year (January–June, July–December).
- Maximum contribution: 10% of base salary or $25,000/year (whichever is less).
- Eligible after 90 days of employment.

2.3 Financial Wellness Program
ACME partners with SmartDollar to provide free access to:
- Online financial planning courses
- Budgeting tools
- 1:1 consultations with a certified financial counsellor (3 sessions/year)

3. PAID TIME OFF

3.1 Vacation
- Years 1–3: 15 days per year
- Years 4–7: 20 days per year
- Years 8+: 25 days per year
- Vacation accrues monthly. Maximum carryover: 10 days into the next calendar year.

3.2 Sick Leave
- 10 days per year (non-accruing, resets January 1).
- Sick days may be used for personal illness, family member illness, or
  medical appointments.
- Unused sick days do not carry over and are not paid out on termination.

3.3 Paid Holidays
ACME observes 11 federal holidays plus 2 floating holidays per year.
Floating holidays may be used on any day with at least 48 hours notice.

3.4 Parental Leave
- Primary caregiver: 16 weeks fully paid parental leave
- Secondary caregiver: 6 weeks fully paid parental leave
- Applies to birth, adoption, and foster placement
- Must be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event
- Intermittent parental leave available with manager and HR approval

3.5 Bereavement Leave
- Immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling): 5 paid days
- Extended family (grandparent, aunt, uncle, in-law): 3 paid days
- Close friend or pet: 1 paid day (at manager discretion)

4. MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLNESS

4.1 Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
All employees and household family members have free access to:
- Up to 8 counselling sessions per year (confidential)
- 24/7 crisis support hotline: 1-800-555-HELP
- Legal consultation (up to 3 sessions/year)
- Financial counselling (up to 3 sessions/year)

4.2 Mental Health Days
Employees may use up to 3 sick days per year specifically as mental health days
without requiring medical documentation.

4.3 Wellness Reimbursement
$600 per year wellness reimbursement for eligible expenses including:
- Gym memberships and fitness classes
- Meditation and mindfulness apps (Calm, Headspace, etc.)
- Ergonomic home office equipment
- Nutrition coaching
Claims submitted via Concur expense system by December 15 each year.

5. LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT

5.1 Learning Budget
Each employee receives $2,000 per year for professional development:
- Online courses (Coursera, LinkedIn Learning, Udemy, etc.)
- Books, journals, and subscriptions
- Professional certifications and exam fees
- Conference attendance (subject to manager approval)

5.2 Tuition Reimbursement
ACME reimburses up to $5,250 per year for degree programmes at accredited
institutions, provided:
- The programme is job-related
- Employee maintains a B average or better
- Employee remains with ACME for 12 months after completing the course
  (pro-rated repayment if leaving earlier)

5.3 Internal Training
All employees have unlimited access to ACME's internal learning library:
- 500+ courses on technical skills, leadership, compliance, and more
- Live instructor-led workshops (quarterly schedule on HR Portal)
- Mentorship matching programme (enroll via HR Portal > Development)

6. ADDITIONAL BENEFITS

6.1 Life Insurance
- Company-paid basic life insurance: 2x annual salary (up to $500,000)
- Optional supplemental life insurance available at employee's cost
- Accidental Death & Dismemberment (AD&D): 1x annual salary

6.2 Commuter Benefits
- Pre-tax transit and parking benefits up to IRS limits ($315/month in 2024)
- Bicycle commuter benefit: $30/month for eligible expenses

6.3 Pet Insurance
Voluntary pet insurance available through Nationwide at group discount rates.
Premiums paid via payroll deduction.

6.4 Employee Discounts
- 20% discount on all ACME products and services
- Discounts on partner services (see benefits portal for full list)

7. BENEFITS ENROLLMENT

7.1 New Hire Enrollment
New employees have 30 days from start date to enroll in benefits.
Coverage is effective on the first day of the month following your start date.
If you miss the enrollment window, you must wait for Open Enrollment.

7.2 Open Enrollment
Open Enrollment occurs annually in November for the following January start.
This is the only time you can change benefits without a qualifying life event.

7.3 Qualifying Life Events
Mid-year changes are permitted within 30 days of a qualifying life event:
- Marriage or divorce
- Birth or adoption of a child
- Death of a dependent
- Spouse losing job-based coverage
- Relocating out of your plan's network area
