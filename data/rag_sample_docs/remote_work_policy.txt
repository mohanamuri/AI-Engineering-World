1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This policy establishes guidelines for remote work arrangements at ACME Corporation.
It applies to all full-time employees, part-time employees, and contractors who
perform work from a location other than a designated ACME office.

The purpose of this policy is to:
- Provide flexibility while maintaining productivity and collaboration
- Ensure data security and compliance when working outside the office
- Set clear expectations for availability, communication, and performance
- Support employee well-being and work-life balance

2. ELIGIBILITY

2.1 Who May Work Remotely
Employees are eligible for remote work if they meet ALL of the following criteria:
- Completed at least 6 months of employment at ACME Corporation
- Demonstrated satisfactory or above performance in the last review cycle
- Hold a role that does not require daily on-site physical presence
- Have a suitable home work environment (private space, reliable internet)

2.2 Roles Excluded from Remote Work
The following roles require full-time on-site presence and are NOT eligible:
- Manufacturing and production floor staff
- Facilities and building management
- On-site security personnel
- Reception and front-desk roles
- Laboratory technicians requiring specialised equipment

2.3 Hybrid vs. Fully Remote
- Hybrid remote: Employee works remotely 2–3 days per week, on-site remainder.
- Fully remote: Employee works entirely remotely. Requires VP-level approval.
- Fully remote employees must visit the nearest ACME office at least once per quarter.

3. APPLICATION PROCESS

3.1 How to Apply
Employees wishing to work remotely must:
1. Complete the Remote Work Request Form (available on HR Portal > Forms).
2. Discuss the arrangement with their direct manager.
3. Obtain written approval from their manager AND HR Business Partner.
4. Complete the Remote Work Security Training module (takes ~45 minutes).
5. Return the signed Remote Work Agreement to HR within 5 business days.

3.2 Trial Period
All new remote work arrangements begin with a 90-day trial period.
During this period either the employee or the manager may terminate
the arrangement with 5 business days notice and no adverse consequences.

3.3 Review and Renewal
Remote work arrangements are reviewed annually during the performance
appraisal cycle. Continued eligibility is not guaranteed and depends on
business needs and individual performance.

4. EXPECTATIONS WHILE WORKING REMOTELY

4.1 Core Hours
All remote employees must be available and responsive during core hours:
- Monday to Friday: 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM (employee's local time zone)
- Employees should communicate their time zone to their manager and team.

4.2 Communication
- Respond to messages via Slack within 2 hours during core hours.
- Respond to email within 4 hours during core hours.
- Attend all scheduled video meetings with camera on unless pre-agreed.
- Proactively communicate blockers, delays, or unexpected unavailability.

4.3 Availability and Calendar
- Keep your calendar up to date with accurate availability.
- Mark out-of-office periods at least 24 hours in advance.
- Do not schedule personal appointments during core hours without manager approval.

4.4 Performance
Remote employees are held to the same performance standards as on-site employees.
Remote work is a privilege that can be revoked if performance declines.

5. EQUIPMENT AND EXPENSES

5.1 Company-Provided Equipment
ACME provides the following equipment for approved remote workers:
- Laptop (standard configuration per role)
- Headset for video conferencing
- Access to required software licences

5.2 Employee-Provided Equipment
Employees are responsible for:
- A suitable desk or workspace
- Reliable broadband internet (minimum 25 Mbps download / 10 Mbps upload)
- A quiet, private area for calls and meetings

5.3 Internet Allowance
Employees working fully remote receive a monthly internet allowance of $50,
paid via the standard expense reimbursement process. Hybrid employees are
not eligible for the internet allowance.

5.4 Office Supplies
A one-time home office setup allowance of $300 is available to employees
approved for full-time remote work. Claims must be submitted within 90 days
of the remote work arrangement start date.

6. DATA SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE

6.1 Security Requirements
Remote employees must comply with all ACME Information Security policies, including:
- Use only ACME-issued devices for work tasks. Personal devices are prohibited
  for accessing confidential company data.
- Connect via the ACME VPN whenever accessing internal systems.
- Never use public Wi-Fi (cafes, airports, hotels) without VPN active.
- Lock your screen when stepping away from your workstation.
- Store all work files on approved cloud platforms (OneDrive, SharePoint).
  Local storage of company data is prohibited.

6.2 Physical Security
- Ensure your workspace is private — no family members or third parties
  should be able to view confidential information on your screen.
- Paper documents with confidential information must be shredded when no
  longer needed. Do not store sensitive paper documents at home long-term.

6.3 Data Breach Response
If you suspect a security incident (lost device, phishing click, unauthorised
access), report it immediately to security@acme.com and your manager.
Do not wait — time is critical in breach response.

7. HEALTH AND SAFETY

ACME is committed to the health and safety of all employees regardless of
work location. Remote employees are responsible for:
- Ensuring their home workspace meets basic ergonomic standards
  (appropriate chair height, monitor at eye level, adequate lighting).
- Taking regular breaks — at least 5 minutes per hour away from the screen.
- Reporting work-related injuries that occur while working from home to HR
  within 24 hours. Home-office injuries may be covered under workers compensation.

ACME provides access to an online ergonomics self-assessment tool via the
HR Portal. Employees experiencing discomfort are encouraged to request an
ergonomics consultation at no cost.

8. POLICY VIOLATIONS

Failure to comply with this policy may result in:
- Revocation of remote work privileges
- Disciplinary action up to and including termination for security violations
- Personal liability for data breaches caused by non-compliance
